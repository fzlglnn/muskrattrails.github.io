Muskrat Trails exists to foster community through the act of riding bicycles, utilizing community trails and spaces, and building events for adventure-minded creatives and wanderers alike.

Our goal is to get outside more, share knowledge related to camping, hiking, and bike maintenance. We hold regular events to engage with other humans face-to-face. Sunday Slow Rides, the bi-annual Muskrat Ramble, bike work shops, and more.

While the modern age encourages humans to move fast, we look to slow it down. Pop into the Muskrat Lodge Discord for the most up-to-date happenings.
